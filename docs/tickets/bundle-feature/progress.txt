## Codebase Patterns

- Schema changes are additive through `schema.sql` on every `store.Open`; cover old-database compatibility with a fixture that predates the new tables.
- Bundle creation validates all members before inserting, then creates generated Options and memberships in one transaction.
- Bundle edits retain member positions; added Items are ordered by checklist insertion and append after the highest retained position.
